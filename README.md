
This repository contains the scripts necessary to peform the analyses reported in [insert title of manuscript]. These analyses bring in and clean field temperature logger data, individually tracked growth measurements, and adult whelk and egg mass surveys conducted in 2021 & 2022. These data, in addition to data from theraml tolerance experiments conducted in the lab, are used to fit models of demographic processes for two species of range-expanding whelks: _Acanthinucella spirata_ and _Mexacanthina lugubris_. Posterior predictive distributions from these demographic models were then used to create posterior distributions of integral projection models, all to understand how these species' population growth rates and underlying demography vary across their home and expanded ranges. The scripts are organized as follows: 

- __1_Clean.Temp.Data.R:__ A script to load in and clean the data from the temperature loggers. Cleaned temperature data is output in the `Outputs` folder as `clean.temp.csv`. A couple figures summarizing the coverage of temperature data are also output in the `Raw_Data_Figures` folder. 
- __2_Compile.Cage.Data.R:__ A script to load in and clean the data from the cage experiment, which is used to fit the individual growth models. Cleaned cage experiment data is output in the `Outputs` folder as `clean.cage.dat.csv`. 
- __3_Compile.Egg.Data.R:__ A script to load in and clean the data from the egg mass surveys, which is used to fit the fecundity models. Cleaned egg mass survey data is output in the `Outputs` folder as `clean.egg.dat.csv`.
- __4_Raw.Data.Figures.R:__ A script that generates a bunch of figures displaying mono and bi-variate distributions of the raw individual growth, survival, and fecundity data. Figures are output in the `Raw_Data_Figures` folder. 
- __5.1_Demo.Mods_Growth.R:__ A script to fit a series of models for individual growth using data from the cage experiment, perform model comparison, and create figures based on the best fitting and most parsimonious model. The fitted model is output in the `Outputs` folder as `growth.mod.RDS`, and the figures are output in the `Demographic_Model_Figures` folder. 
- __5.2_Demo.Mods_Survival.R:__ A script to fit a series of models for survival using data from the laboratory thermal tolerance experiment, perform model comparison, and create figures based on the best fitting and most parsimonious model. The fitted model is output in the `Outputs` folder as the `surv.mod.RDS`, and the figures are output in the `Demographic_Model_Figures` folder. 
- __5.3_Demo.Mods_Fecundity.R:__ A script to fit a series of models for per capita egg capsule production using data from the field surveys of egg masses, perform model comparison, and create figures based on the best fitting and most parsimonious model. The fitted model is output in the `Outputs` folder as the `fec.mod.RDS`, and the figures are output in the `Demographic_Model_Figures` folder. 
- __6_Calculate.Growth.Rates.R:__ A script to extract the posterior (predictive) distributions from the fitted demographic models and construct integral projection models for both species at all sites across a discrete range of temperature values. Population growth rates are then calculated from these IPMs and compared with the abundance data from the field surveys. Figures are produced describing variation in population growth rates and correspondance with empirical survey data and are output in the `Population_Model_Figures`. 
- __7_Sensitivity.Analysis.R:__ A script to conduct a sensitivity/elasticity analysis of the median IPM's per species. Some figures are output in the `Population_Model_Figures`.
- __8_Recruitment.R:__ A script to construct IPMs and calculate population growth rates across a gradient of assumed, fixed recruitment rate values.
